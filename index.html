About Me

Art isn't what I do.
It's how I see.

Hi, I'm Divyanshi — a third-year BFA Painting student at AAFT Noida, with a foundation in Commercial Arts from Women Residential Polytechnic College. I specialize in mixed media and portraiture, blending traditional techniques with contemporary storytelling.

From painting large-scale murals on walls to crafting aesthetic reels that stop the scroll — I bring the same creative energy to every medium I touch. I believe the best content is created by hands that understand craft, not tools that imitate it.

Why Me

The story behind
the craft

I started painting portraits because I was obsessed with capturing emotion in a face — the way eyes tell entire stories. That same obsession drove me to walls, to canvases, to phone screens. Wherever I can tell a story, I will.

At AAFT Noida, I discovered that art isn't just about beautiful things — it's about communication. That's when I started creating content. My videos aren't just aesthetic — they're intentional. My designs aren't just pretty — they have a point of view.

I don't know how to stop creating. It's not discipline for me — it's just who I am.
